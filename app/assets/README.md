# Display assets

Platform marks: pinned Simple Icons 15.20.0, CC0-1.0; source URLs and brand colors are in sources.json. Brand marks remain the property of their owners. No endorsement is implied.

Other icons and placeholder layouts: original simple vector drawings for this repository, under the repository license. Platform marks retain brand color. The rendering path centers them on a pale square with padding so they remain readable on light and dark Notion backgrounds. Cards preserve content aspect ratio. These are display copies and never replace archived originals.

2026-10-02: the nine `placeholder-*.svg` assets describe content categories: paper, article, tutorial, document, post, image, video, audio, other. Each is an opaque 600×400 canvas with a larger, lightly filled illustration and a category color. They contain no visible titles or platform marks. Website/platform logos are page icons only. The paper illustration has an actual chart and text columns; nonacademic PDFs can use document/tutorial art. The gallery preview is in `c7973d4:docs/fallback-category-preview-2026-10-02.png`.
